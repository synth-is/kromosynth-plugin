(bthj, comment on the bead). It settles what this repository may say; it does
not replace the Synth.is terms of service, which stay authoritative for the
hosted service.

## 1. Renders and artefacts a user evolved or downloaded

- Synth.is claims **no copyright** in a sound rendered from a user's account
  or in the `.dsp` / `.wasm` evolved for that user. The user may use them
  commercially, modify and redistribute them, with no attribution required.
- Artefacts are provided **as is**, without warranty. The AGPL is **not**
  claimed over them: it covers the code in this repository only, and running
  an artefact in the plugin does not make it AGPL.
- Third-party terms still apply underneath. The generated code incorporates
  Faust library code under that library's own licence, and its compiler is
  the Faust compiler. Those licences could not be verified from this
  repository. The working assumption, to confirm against the upstream Faust
  licence pages before the first public release, is that they permit use of
  the generated output in any product. If a library turns out to require
  notice, it is added to the artefact header and to THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

## 2. The twelve modules embedded in `tools/ios-gate0/src/Gate0Data.c`

They are benchmark fixtures for the iOS gate-0 probe, are not part of the
plugin or the installer, and are offered on the terms of section 1 (no
Synth.is copyright claimed, as is, upstream Faust licences unchanged). The
repository makes no further claim about them.

## 3. Name, logo and app icon

- "Synth.is", its logo and `resources/AppIcon-1024.png` (generated by
  `resources/make-appicon.py` from the designer's branding folder) are brand
  assets, **not** licensed by the AGPL.
- They are used here with the designer's permission for the official
  Synth.is builds only. A fork or redistribution must replace the icon and
  must not present itself as Synth.is. Nominative reference ("based on
  Synth.is") is fine.
- The designer's rights in the AppIcon artwork are held by the designer and
  Synth.is, not by contributors; the file is excluded from the AGPL grant.
